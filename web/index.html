Rail Runner

A miner runs along the three rails of a mine: catch the carts full of gold, diamonds and gems, and stay away from the TNT. This page has a CPC emulator built in, so there is nothing to install.

  • Enterstart
  • Iinstructions
  • O↑jump up a rail
  • P↓duck down a rail
  • Rretry the level
  • Qback to the menu
